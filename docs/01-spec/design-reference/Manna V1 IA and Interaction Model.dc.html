Supplied words — proposed resolution R5 · OPEN IN THE FIXTURE RECORD

Matthew 4:15 in the Pure Cambridge Edition marks [by] as supplied by the translators. The accepted prototype drops the marking, which silently promotes a translator's word to translated text. The proposal: carry the distinction in the data model, render it as italic — the KJV's own print convention — and state the convention once in the reader legend rather than per verse.
