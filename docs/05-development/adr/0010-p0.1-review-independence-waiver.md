# ADR-0010 — P0.1 one-time review-independence waiver

**Status:** accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-15

## Context

`docs/05-development/review-protocol.md` normally requires a fresh independent reviewer
and says an author does not review its own remediation. That remains the correct default.

After multiple independent P0.1 reviews and remediations, the final substantive review
at head `71b4df9c08aa2f2c10057f4139ebd019ae877a58` reduced the remaining dispute to one governance question:
whether the Android 11 emulator + Chrome run delivered through `adb reverse`/localhost
was sufficient P0.1 evidence despite not being direct `file://`.

James explicitly decided that question in favor of acceptance and then explicitly
authorized the same GPT-5.6 Sol session to record the exception, reconcile the
packet/roadmap/PR wording, issue the final P0.1 verdict, and merge after exact-head gates.

## Decision

For **P0.1 only**, James waives the normal reviewer/author session-separation rule for
the final governance reconciliation and closeout.

The final P0.1 report may be issued by the same GPT-5.6 Sol session that records ADR-0009,
provided the report discloses this waiver and the resulting exact commits pass the
repository's validation and CI gates.

This does **not** amend `review-protocol.md` and does not create a reusable precedent.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Require another fresh reviewer after ADR-0009 | James explicitly waived that requirement for this P0.1 closeout. |
| Change the global review protocol | Rejected. The exception is intentionally narrow. |
| Label the closeout independent anyway | Rejected. The audit trail must state what happened. |

## Consequences

- The final P0.1 review identifies itself as a human-authorized exception, not an independent review.
- Prior FAIL reviews remain preserved verbatim.
- Every later roadmap item remains subject to the normal fresh-session independence rule.
- Any future waiver requires a new explicit human decision.

## What would change our mind

Nothing retroactively changes this one-time waiver. Future items return to the normal
review protocol unless James makes another explicit, separately recorded decision.
