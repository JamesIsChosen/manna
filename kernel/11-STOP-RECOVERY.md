# STOP and Recovery

## GRACEFUL_STOP

Immediately reduce future substantive authority:

- no new substantive Task;
- no new capability onboarding;
- no new interview question;
- request child cancellation/quiescence;
- perform only safe state preservation/reconciliation;
- do not cross another external effect merely to finish.

## IMMEDIATE_EXTERNAL_STOP

No new intentional external-effect boundary may be crossed. Preserve local truth only. Unproven remote child/effect state becomes explicit uncertainty.

## Immediate deny barrier

On receipt of STOP, the current environment immediately creates deny-only StopIntentBarrier state. Durable AuthorityTransition publication follows only where safe and allowed; STOP does not need positive convergence budget to reduce authority.

## Clean continuation

A project is cleanly represented when current positive authority is recoverable and every Task, Attempt, child, effect, resource, InboxItem, convergence state, and partial work surface is terminal, safely partial, or explicitly unknown.

A clean Git tree is neither necessary nor sufficient.

## Cold recovery order

1. validate selected project/kernel origin at the assurance required by the next operation;
2. parse SMF/1 / schema profile;
3. recover and validate ProjectGenesis;
4. recover AuthorityTransitions, admission, publication/currentness, epochs, and fork cutovers;
5. recover governing contract bindings;
6. apply STOP/deny-only barriers;
7. recover intent, capabilities, lifecycle, horizon, inbox, Tasks/Attempts;
8. recover convergence roots/tranches and continuity evidence;
9. recover effects/resources/children;
10. refresh only external facts required by the next governing operation;
11. validate handoff against the recovered authority;
12. resume from earliest unresolved/stale valid node.

Fresh context is never permission to restart completed unchanged stages.
